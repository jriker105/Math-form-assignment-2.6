4. A continuous-flow automated processing plant utilizes a primary cylindrical holding tank with a constant radius of R = 3 meters and a steady fluid height of H = 10 meters. Pure solvent is pumped into the top of the tank at a constant volumetric flow rate of Vin = 2 m3/min. Concurrently, a perfectly mixed solution exits the bottom of the tank through a drainage valve at an identical rate of Vout = 2 m3/min. At time t = 0 minutes, a mechanical valve failure releases a mass of M0 = 15 kg of highly soluble dye directly into the tank, distributing uniformly throughout the volume instantly. Concurrently, a heating array at the base causes the solvent to evaporate at a constant rate of E = 0.5 m3/min (the resulting vapor contains 0% dye concentration). To prevent volume loss, a secondary automated valve introduces an auxiliary stream of pure solvent at exactly 0.5 m3/min, keeping the fluid height perfectly constant. Liquid exiting the main drainage valve immediately enters a straight, horizontal pipeline of length L = 60 meters and a uniform cross-sectional area of A = 0.1 m2. The fluid exhibits perfect plug flow behavior through this pipe (zero axial mixing, zero friction, and a uniform velocity profile).

  • (b) Determine the exact time tmax (in minutes, measured from t = 0) at which the total mass of the dye present inside the boundaries of the 60-meter downstream pipeline reaches its absolute maximum value. Justify your answer using optimization principles or transport logic. [15 marks]
